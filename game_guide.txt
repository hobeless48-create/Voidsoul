=============================================================
🌟 VOID SOUL: GAME GUIDE & MECHANICS 🌟
=============================================================

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for Void Soul! This document
explains the intricate details of the game, from basic flows to
advanced combat mechanics.

=============================================================
📖 1. THE MYSTERY & GOAL
=============================================================
In the shadowed annals of Eldrath, ancient kingdoms cling to a 
fragile peace. The Glimmering Woods, once a passage between realms, 
has been corrupted. Shadow magic seeps from long-sealed catacombs, 
twisting beasts and birthing horrors. 

Your ultimate goal: Assemble a party of heroes, clear the perilous
stages, navigate the corrupted lands, and defeat the Lich King in 
the Cursed Catacombs to save the world from eternal darkness!

=============================================================
🔄 2. HOW THE GAME WORKS (GAME FLOW)
=============================================================
The core loop of the game is:
LOBBY ➡️ MAP ➡️ BATTLE ➡️ CAMP ➡️ MAP ... ➡️ VICTORY

• 🏰 LOBBY: Form your party (up to 4 heroes), manage talents and gear.
• 🗺️ MAP: Choose your path! Pick between combat, elite combat, events, 
        or merchant nodes.
• ⚔️ BATTLE: Turn-based tactical combat.
• 🏕️ CAMP: Rest to heal, craft gear, buy/sell, and stash gold.
• 🎭 PROMOTION: At levels 15, 30, 50, and 75, characters advance 
             to more powerful classes!

=============================================================
⚔️ 3. COMBAT DETAILS: SKILLS, DAMAGE & DEFENSE
=============================================================
Combat happens on a 3x3 grid (Frontline, Midline, Backline).

• 💥 Damage: 
  Calculated using your class's base scaling (e.g., Warrior uses STR/CON, 
  Mage uses INT) multiplied by the Skill's multiplier.

• 🛡️ Defense (DEF) & Block:
  DEF reduces incoming damage flatly (minimum 1 damage).
  Block (based on STR/CON) reduces damage further. 
  Shields absorb damage before HP. Guard stance halves incoming damage!

• 💨 Dodge & Accuracy:
  Dodge is capped at 30%. Accuracy counters high-dodge enemies.

• ✨ Skills & Status Effects:
  Use Energy to cast skills. Apply powerful status effects:
  🔥 Burn (Damage over time)
  🌿 Poison (Damage over time)
  🩸 Bleed (Damage over time)
  🛡️ Taunt (Forces enemies to attack you)
  💫 Stagger/Stun (Skips turn)

=============================================================
🗺️ 4. CHAPTERS & EVENTS
=============================================================
• 🏔️ Chapters:
  Each chapter consists of 5 areas (Forest has 8). You must clear the 
  boss at the end to advance. The path branches (e.g., Cave vs Dungeon).

• 🎲 Events (>70 Unique Encounters):
  You will encounter mysteries, goblin ambushes, and strange rituals.
  You make choices that involve Stat Checks (e.g., High STR to lift a boulder).
  - Success: Rewards (Gold, XP, Items, Buffs)
  - Failure: Consequences (Damage, Debuffs, Forced Combat)

=============================================================
🔨 5. CRAFTING & ITEMS
=============================================================
• 🎒 Equipment:
  10 Slots: Weapon, Offhand, Helmet, Chest, Legs, Boots, Ring, 
  Bracelet, Necklace, Charm.

• 🧪 Crafting:
  Collect materials from battles and events to craft powerful gear at the Camp!
  
  Common Materials include:
  🪨 Iron Ore        🐺 Wolf Pelt
  🌸 Magic Bloom     🦴 Ancient Bone
  💎 Voidstone       🩸 Demon Blood

  Combine them at the Camp menu to create powerful weapons, armors, and
  consumables like healing potions!
