In the shadowed annals of Eldrath, where ancient kingdoms cling to fragile peace, a band of unlikely heroes first crossed paths amid the clamor of the frontier town of Thornvale. Drawn together by fate and necessity—a wandering warrior seeking lost honor, a sharp-eyed rogue chasing rumors of forgotten treasure, a devout cleric answering a divine calling, and others bound by their own secrets—they formed a fragile pact.
Whispers had spread through the taverns: the Glimmering Woods, once a verdant passage between realms, now lay corrupted. Shadow magic seeped from long-sealed catacombs beneath its roots, twisting beasts and birthing horrors. Caravans vanished, villages trembled, and the king’s call for brave souls carried a handsome reward. With little coin and fewer choices, the party shouldered their packs and stepped beneath the ancient canopy.
The trees here glowed with an eerie, ethereal light, their leaves shimmering like captured starlight. Yet beneath the beauty lurked unease. Ancient trunks groaned warnings carried on the wind, while distant goblin laughter echoed through the mist. This was no ordinary forest—it was a trial, a living threshold where light and shadow waged an eternal war.
Little did they know, their first steps into the Glimmering Woods would set in motion a grand adventure that would test their bonds, awaken hidden powers, and decide the fate of realms beyond...
