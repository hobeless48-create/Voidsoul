================================================================================
                  VOID SOUL / AN AVERAGE CAMPAIGN
              100 EVENTS & ENCOUNTERS — REVISED FOR GAME SYSTEMS
================================================================================
Star Difficulty Rating
1★ = Mundane / Safe
2★ = Minor risk or roleplay
3★ = Moderate challenge
4★ = Hard / Dangerous
5★ = Epic / Potentially campaign-altering

================================================================================
TRAVEL & EXPLORATION (1–3★ mostly)
================================================================================

(1★) Peaceful roadside shrine – a Cleric NPC sells minor blessings (+5 FTH for
     next battle) and cheap healing potions. Cleric/High Priest PCs get a free
     Regen buff (value 5, 2 turns).

(1★) Abandoned merchant caravan with spoiled rations and a few copper coins.
     Searching it yields 1x Wolf Pelt and a broken Iron Shield (not equippable,
     but salvageable for 2x Iron Ore at camp).

(2★) A traveling minstrel has composed an "epic ballad" about the party —
     uncomfortably accurate in places. [CHA check] A Bard PC who passes can
     Dissonance the crowd's laughter into awe, granting +10% party ATK for the
     next combat. On fail, the party is Crippled (-25% damage) from embarrassment
     for 1 turn.

(2★) Dense fog rolls across the map node. The party must choose their lane
     blind — the fog hides whether it leads to a campground, elite combat, or
     merchant. [DEX check] Rogues or Archers with DEX 45+ can scout ahead and
     reveal the true node type before committing.

(2★) Ruined village market — scavengers are trading salvaged goods at high
     prices. Iron Ore costs 2x gold here. However, a Rogue PC with LCK 40+
     can pickpocket 1 free crafting material (random) without triggering a
     combat encounter.

(1★) A small general store in a surviving hamlet sells weapons, rations, and
     common items. Restores the camp Feast phase early — the party may heal
     30% max HP right now instead of waiting for the next campground node.

(1★) A blacksmith offers basic armor repairs. All equipped Chestplate and
     Helmet bonuses are refreshed. Warriors, Knights, and Berserkers get a
     free +5 DEF bonus applied until the next battle.

(2★) Mystic herb shop — glowing Magic Bloom variants line the shelves. Each
     PC may drink one for a random buff: Regen (value 10, 3 turns), +15% crit
     chance, or +20 DEF for 2 battles. Roll randomly; side effects include a
     1-turn Stagger at the start of the next combat if the roll is odd.

(3★) Corrupted ley line crossing — arcane energy warps the 3x3 grid at the
     start of the next battle. All characters are randomly repositioned at
     combat start. [INT check] Mage or Elementalist PCs who pass may choose
     their own starting column instead of being moved randomly.

(2★) A refugee caravan needs escort to the next safe node. Successfully
     escorting them (no PC drops to 0 HP during the escort combat) rewards
     the party with 3x Ancient Bone and 30 gold. If a PC gains a Fallen
     Stack during the escort, the refugees scatter and the reward is lost.

================================================================================
SOCIAL & ROLEPLAY EVENTS
================================================================================

(2★) A grieving noble begs the party to find his lost daughter. [CHA check]
     A Bard PC using Valor Anthem as a "rallying speech" auto-passes. Success
     opens a side quest node; failure means the noble offers a lesser reward
     (only gold, no crafting materials).

(3★) A corrupt lord accuses the party of being demon spies and orders his
     guards to attack. The battle begins with enemies in Taunt formation —
     all guards target whoever stands in Column 1 (Frontline). Warriors and
     Knights positioned there take focused fire immediately.

(2★) A tavern debate about whether the Demon Lord is real grows heated. A
     Bard PC can use Dissonance to break up the argument peacefully. Failing
     the [CHA check] triggers a bar brawl — a low-stakes combat with
     Stagger-heavy enemies but no Fallen Stack risk.

(1★) A friendly inn with warm food and sturdy beds. The party enters the
     Feast phase for free: heal 30% max HP, craft one item using available
     materials, and deposit gold into the shared stash.

(3★) A cultist disguised as a priest attempts to recruit the party by
     offering "holy relics" (actually void-cursed items). [FTH check]
     A Cleric or High Priest PC can expose the ruse with Purify, causing the
     cultist to flee and dropping 2x Ancient Bone. On fail, one PC equips a
     cursed Charm that applies Burn (value 8) at the start of every battle
     until removed at a proper shop.

(4★) A survivor of a destroyed city recognizes one PC's fighting style from
     the invasion. Combat begins mid-conversation — the survivor summons
     militia. All militia units spawn in Column 1 (Frontline) and immediately
     Taunt your front-row character. The PC in question starts the battle with
     the Vulnerable status (+50% damage taken, 2 turns).

(3★) The ghost of a fallen king appears over the campground node, warning of
     betrayal from within. No combat — but one random PC's talent tree node
     is locked for the next battle (treat as if that node is unequipped).
     [FTH check] A Cleric PC using Holy Light can put the ghost to rest and
     restore the locked node.

(2★) Children in a village square want the party to play "Heroes vs Demons."
     A Warrior PC can volunteer for the role of "dungeon boss" — doing so
     grants the whole party a Warcry buff (+15% ATK) as a morale bonus going
     into the next combat.

(4★) The High Priestess has a vision that one party member "resonates with an
     old enemy's soul." That PC's aggro weight is doubled for the next battle
     (enemies treat them as if their base aggro is ×2), regardless of class or
     position.

(3★) An Alliance officer delivers new orders with suspicious enthusiasm. He
     insists the party take an "elite" combat node rather than the campground.
     [CHA check] A Bard or Bard of Valor PC using Heroic Symphony can talk
     the officer down and preserve the campground node option.

================================================================================
COMBAT ENCOUNTERS
================================================================================

(2★) Bandit ambush on the road. Bandits prioritize Column 1 targets. Their
     leader is Crippled (-25% damage) if any PC used a Taunt skill on the
     previous turn going into this event.

(3★) A pack of corrupted wolves with demonic mutations. Two wolves enter
     Vulnerable (+50% damage taken) when Bleed is applied. Rogues with the
     Hemorrhage talent deal devastating damage here. One wolf per turn attempts
     to Stagger a Column 1 character.

(3★) Skeletal knights guard a fallen castle. They are immune to Bleed and
     Poison. Burn, Holy damage (FTH scaling), and Mage AoE skills are the
     recommended approach. Skeletons in the front row use Guard stance,
     halving incoming damage and doubling their Block Chance.

(4★) An Archdemon lieutenant — a vaguely familiar face — leads a raid.
     He targets whichever PC has the highest STR stat first. He opens the
     battle with a Vulnerable debuff on that PC. Defeating him drops 4x Iron
     Ore and a rare weapon.

(2★) Goblin raiders using stolen Alliance weapons. Their accuracy is poor
     (base 60%) but they apply Stagger on critical hits. Rogues and Archers
     can Shadow Step or reposition out of their attack column to avoid most
     damage.

(3★) Possessed townsfolk turn violent mid-conversation. They apply Bleed on
     hit and enter a Berserk phase (Vulnerable to all damage, +30% ATK) when
     below 30% HP. [FTH check] A Cleric using Purify on a townsfolk enemy
     instantly incapacitates one without dealing damage.

(4★) A demon-tainted dragon wyrmling occupies the Column 1–2 enemy cells.
     Its breath attack hits the entire front column of allies with Burn (value
     12, 2 turns). Warriors in Guard stance reduce this to value 6. Mages
     using Mana Shield or Glacial Lance are especially valuable here.

(5★) A rival hero party — failed experiments — charges the battlefield
     screaming. Each enemy mirrors one PC's class (Warrior vs Warrior, Mage vs
     Mage, etc.) and opens with that class's signature skill. They cannot be
     Taunted. Defeating them drops one crafting material matching the mirrored
     PC's primary stat gear.

(3★) Elemental spirits corrupted by void energy. They rotate resistances each
     turn: fire → physical → magic → holy. [INT check] A Mage or Elementalist
     with Elemental Mastery talent can read the rotation and gain +20% damage
     by targeting the active weakness.

(4★) Valthorak's elite hell-knights ambush the party. Two hell-knights occupy
     Frontline Column 1 and immediately use Guard stance. A third spawns in
     Column 2 with the Taunt status targeting your highest-FTH PC. Killing all
     three within 5 turns rewards a bonus Ancient Bone drop.

================================================================================
SHOP & ECONOMY EVENTS
================================================================================

(1★) A traveling potion vendor sells Healing Potions (restore 40 HP), Mana
     Vials (restore 2 EN), and Antidotes (cleanse Burn/Poison/Bleed). Bard PCs
     using Fortune's Tune before purchasing grant the party +10% chance to find
     a rare item in the vendor's stock.

(1★) Magic item auction in a neutral free city. The party may bid on a random
     piece of gear. Rogue PCs with LCK 50+ may "acquire" one item without
     bidding, bypassing the gold cost entirely.

(1★) A black market dealer sells "cursed" relics. Each relic is actually a
     plot-relevant Charm or Necklace slot item that grants a strong passive
     but applies a minor DoT (Poison, value 3) to the wearer each battle.

(2★) An enchanter offers to upgrade a weapon. The upgrade adds +20% scaling to
     the weapon's primary stat. [INT check] On failure, the weapon gains the
     Void Corruption property: the wielder takes Burn (value 5) whenever they
     crit. A Mage PC auto-passes the INT check.

(1★) General store in a walled town. Standard crafting materials available:
     Iron Ore, Wolf Pelt, Magic Bloom, Ancient Bone. Prices are standard.
     Warriors and Knights get a 10% discount on Iron Ore due to their
     reputation.

(2★) Exotic goods from fallen kingdoms — rare ingredients including double
     batches of Magic Bloom and Ancient Bone. These can be used to craft
     high-tier items like the Archmage Scepter or Talisman of Power at camp.

(3★) The shopkeeper is a devil in disguise. He offers deals that are genuinely
     excellent — but purchasing anything triggers a combat encounter as his
     true form is revealed. The combat starts with the party Staggered for
     turn 1. All purchased goods are kept regardless of combat outcome.

(1★) An armorsmith specializes in holy-symbol engravings. Any Shield or
     Chestplate equipped by a Cleric or High Priest gains +10 FTH scaling for
     the next two battles if purchased here.

(2★) An artifact merchant claims a necklace "hums when near true evil." It
     functions as a Charm slot item granting +15 LCK. However, at the start
     of any battle involving a demon-type enemy, it pulses — revealing which
     column the highest-threat enemy occupies before initiative is rolled.

(4★) Demons assault the shop while the party is browsing. The battlefield is
     the shop interior — all characters begin in Column 3 (Farthest Back) and
     must advance. Cover objects grant Guard stance passively for turn 1 only.
     Surviving the assault lets the party loot 3x Iron Ore and 2x Wolf Pelt
     free of charge.

================================================================================
MYSTERY & INVESTIGATION
================================================================================

(3★) Ruins in the area bear strange symbols that match one PC's birthmark
     exactly. [INT check] A Mage or Elementalist who passes identifies it as
     an arcane seal — the PC's Mana Shield capacity is doubled for the next
     battle, but they also begin that battle with the Vulnerable status as the
     seal flares.

(4★) An ancient library contains records of the party's "heroic" deeds — and
     earlier, far darker ones. One PC loses their turn 1 action in the next
     combat (Stagger, as the revelation hits them mid-battle). [FTH check]
     A Cleric using Miracle can stabilize the PC and negate this penalty.

(3★) A captured demon laughs and calls a PC by a name they don't recognize.
     That PC's aggro weight is treated as maximum (40 + position modifier) for
     the rest of this combat node, as if they were a frontline Warrior.

(5★) A memory crystal activates and shows the party's original Archdemon forms
     destroying a capital. Every PC begins the next combat with 1 Fallen Stack
     already applied (-25% damage dealt, -25% healing received). A Bard of
     Valor using Heroic Symphony before the battle can negate one Fallen Stack
     per affected PC.

(4★) A traitor inside the Alliance has been feeding intel to the demons. The
     next combat node's enemy formation is revealed in advance — but the trap
     is that the enemies also know the party's formation. Enemies will
     pre-target whichever PC has the lowest DEF at battle start.

(3★) Every villager in a small town shares the same nightmare about the party.
     [CHA check] A Bard performing Soothing Lute for the villagers calms them.
     On fail, the party rests here but receives no HP from the Feast (the
     disturbed atmosphere prevents proper rest).

(2★) A stone prophecy tablet is found with lines that contradict the official
     Alliance history. One line mentions a specific class by name. The PC of
     that class gains +1 free Talent Point, but the tablet crumbles before
     more can be read.

(5★) The party discovers their own demonic armor and weapons, preserved in a
     sealed vault. Each piece of equipment is a perfect upgrade of whatever
     that PC currently wears (+15 to all scaling stats). Equipping the items
     applies a permanent Burn DoT (value 5) each battle that cannot be
     cleansed — until a key story event occurs.

(4★) A captured Archdemon recognizes the party as "siblings" and offers to
     reveal the next chapter's elite enemy formations — if one PC willingly
     takes a Fallen Stack as proof of trust. [CHA check] A Bard PC can bluff
     the information out without taking the stack.

(3★) Historical records show timeline discrepancies that imply the party
     existed before the official founding of the Alliance. [INT check] A Mage
     or Elementalist who passes gains a clue item. On fail, an archivist
     alerted by the party's questions sends guards — a 2★ combat encounter
     triggers.

================================================================================
SIDE QUESTS & PERSONAL ARCS
================================================================================

(2★) A farmer needs help clearing undead from his land. The undead are immune
     to Bleed and Poison. Clerics and High Priests deal +30% damage to these
     enemies via holy scaling. Completing the quest rewards 2x Ancient Bone.

(3★) Retrieve a sacred relic from a haunted temple. The dungeon node counts
     as two consecutive combat encounters with no campground between them.
     The relic itself functions as a Charm slot item: +20 FTH and +1 EN
     regeneration per battle.

(4★) Escort a merchant caravan through demon-infested territory across three
     consecutive combat nodes. After each node, one random PC loses 10% max HP
     (exhaustion) unless a Cleric uses Regenerate on them. Completing all
     three nodes without a PC reaching 0 HP rewards a Talisman of Power recipe
     unlock (crafted for half the usual materials).

(3★) Crops near old battlefields are failing — void crystals are leeching
     nutrients. The combat here involves Elemental spirits (see encounter
     above). Cleansing all void crystals (Purify used 3 times in combat)
     rewards 3x Magic Bloom and a +5 FTH bonus to any Cleric in the party.

(2★) Deliver medicine to a plague-stricken town. No combat, but the party
     must not use any healing potions or Feast HP recovery for the journey —
     the medicine must arrive intact. On success, the town's healer teaches
     one Cleric or High Priest PC the Blessed Aura talent node for free.

(4★) A rogue demon keeps calling one PC "master" and refuses to fight back.
     The party must decide: finish it (combat encounter, enemy has 0 aggro and
     never attacks), spare it (it joins as a passive camp NPC who identifies
     one void-cursed item per chapter), or interrogate it (grants 5★ lore
     clues but triggers a 3★ demon ambush immediately after).

(5★) One PC's "false memory family" is found — actually villagers from a town
     the party destroyed in a past life. They recognize the PC. That PC begins
     the next combat with Vulnerable (+50% damage taken) and Crippled (-25%
     damage dealt) as guilt manifests. The only cure is a Bard using Grand
     Finale to emotionally resolve the scene.

(3★) A tournament in the capital celebrates the heroes. PCs enter 1v1 duels
     in the 3x3 arena. Win three consecutive duels to earn a rare weapon (the
     Gilded Broadsword or Longbow of Gales, winner's choice). Losses give the
     PC a Fallen Stack (tournament rules — no death, but exhaustion counts).

(4★) A town votes to execute suspected demon collaborators who are actually
     innocent. [CHA check] A Bard of Valor using Heroic Symphony can sway the
     crowd. On fail, the party must intervene in combat — fighting town guards
     who Taunt the highest-STR PC immediately. Saving the innocents rewards
     a Phoenix Feather crafting material.

(5★) During combat, one PC begins having flashbacks. At the start of their
     turn each round, roll a chance (50%) that they act on instinct — using
     their highest-damage skill against a random target (ally or enemy). A
     Cleric using Purify or a Bard using Rallying Chorus on that PC ends the
     flashback state.

================================================================================
MAJOR PLOT PROGRESSION EVENTS
================================================================================

(3★) First meeting with Alliance leaders. [CHA check] A Bard or Bard of Valor
     PC who passes unlocks a passive Alliance Blessing: +10 to all party DEF
     for the current chapter. On fail, the Alliance assigns a distrustful
     officer who reduces the party's camp Feast HP recovery to 20% instead
     of 30%.

(4★) The party acquires the first Holy Relic. It occupies the Charm slot and
     grants +25 FTH to the holder. Holy damage skills used by any PC in the
     party gain +15% potency while the relic is carried.

(4★) Assault on a demon outpost. The outpost has a fortified Column 1 row of
     enemies, all in Guard stance at battle start. AoE skills (Fireball,
     Discordant Wail, Titan's Wrath) are especially effective here. Clearing
     the outpost drops 4x Iron Ore and 3x Ancient Bone.

(5★) Confrontation with a minor Demon Lord serving Valthorak. This boss applies
     a party-wide Vulnerable debuff on turn 1. All damage-reduction effects
     (Guard, Block, Mana Shield) still apply on top of Vulnerable. Surviving
     turn 1 without a KO awards a bonus loot drop.

(4★) Discovery of Zorath the Soulforger's hidden laboratory. Scattered notes
     function as free Talent Point unlocks — one per PC (can be applied to any
     node they could legally reach). The lab also contains 2x Magic Bloom and
     an Archmage Scepter blueprint.

(5★) The rival hero party returns — now twisted demonic versions of themselves.
     They have all the same skills as before, plus each one applies Burn (value
     10) on hit. Bards of Chaos using Hexing Chord can reduce their ATK by 30%,
     making survival much more manageable.

(5★) Full memory restoration event — one PC recalls the truth first. For the
     remainder of this chapter, that PC's damage is increased by 20% (clarity
     and fury combined), but they start every battle with 1 Fallen Stack as
     the weight of memory takes its toll.

(5★) Alliance forces turn against the party after evidence surfaces. The party
     faces three back-to-back combat nodes with no campground. Fallen Stacks
     carry over. A PC with 3 Fallen Stacks risks permanent death if they reach
     0 HP in any of these nodes.

(5★) Valthorak offers a direct audience — and offers to reveal the full truth
     if the party switches sides. Choosing to listen grants the party a free
     chapter skip (skip to next chapter's first node) but applies 1 Fallen
     Stack to every PC from the psychic strain of his presence.

(5★) Final choice: continue serving Zorath, betray him, or attempt redemption.
     Each path alters chapter 4's lane count and enemy difficulty. Betraying
     Zorath reduces lane count to 8 (easier) but triggers an immediate 5★
     boss encounter as his constructs attack. Redemption path keeps 10 lanes
     but unlocks a unique Talent node for all PCs: "Soul's Reckoning."

================================================================================
HIGH DIFFICULTY / SPECIAL EVENTS (4–5★)
================================================================================

(4★) A void storm blankets the map node. Divine magic is suppressed — all FTH
     scaling is halved and Cleric/High Priest skills cost +1 EN to activate.
     The storm lifts after 3 turns of combat. Mage and Rogue PCs are unaffected.

(5★) Battle inside a collapsing pocket dimension. Each turn, one random grid
     cell (ally or enemy side) is destroyed. Characters standing on a destroyed
     cell are forced to Column 3. Warriors and Knights who cannot legally stand
     on Column 3 are instead Staggered for 1 turn when displaced.

(4★) The party fights shadow clones of their past demonic selves. Each clone
     mirrors the corresponding PC's current stats exactly — including Fallen
     Stacks, equipped gear, and active talent nodes. The only advantage: clones
     cannot use Bard support skills on each other.

(5★) Moral event: a powerful artifact can level a city in exchange for enough
     void energy to defeat Valthorak in one hit. Choosing to use it eliminates
     the final boss's HP threshold mechanic — but every PC gains 2 Fallen
     Stacks permanently and one PC's talent tree is wiped entirely.

(4★) A trusted NPC companion betrays the party. They enter the battlefield on
     the enemy side — with full knowledge of the party's grid positioning,
     talent trees, and aggro weights. They specifically target whoever has the
     most Fallen Stacks.

(5★) The party's demonic forms begin manifesting involuntarily. At the start of
     each battle, each PC has a 25% chance to begin in a Berserk state:
     +50% damage dealt, but they target randomly (ally or enemy). A Cleric
     using Purify on a Berserk PC restores their control immediately.

(4★) Siege of a major surviving city. The party defends across two consecutive
     3★ combats and one 4★ elite combat. After each combat, one PC is randomly
     assigned to "wall duty" and starts the next battle in Column 3 regardless
     of preference. Warriors and Knights displaced this way are Staggered turn 1.

(5★) Zorath appears in a vision at the campground and speaks the party's true
     names. Each PC's highest talent tree node is temporarily unlocked for the
     next battle for free — even if they haven't purchased the prerequisite
     nodes. After the battle, the nodes revert.

(4★) Hunt for Zorath's experimental notes across a dungeon node. Three
     consecutive combats, each dropping one page. Collecting all three pages
     unlocks a new crafting recipe: Steel Aegis at half material cost.

(5★) Climactic battle against Valthorak while fighting internal corruption.
     Every 2 turns, one random PC enters Berserk (see above). Valthorak
     himself targets whichever PC has the lowest current HP each turn, ignoring
     Taunt entirely. Fallen Stacks accumulated here carry into the ending
     cutscene — affecting which ending variant is displayed.

================================================================================
MISCELLANEOUS / FILLER EVENTS
================================================================================

(2★) Festival celebrating the heroes. The party enters a free Feast phase.
     A Bard PC who performs here (any Bard skill used narratively) grants the
     party a 3-turn Regen buff (value 6) going into the next combat — "the
     crowd's cheers still ringing in their ears."

(1★) A peaceful fishing village offers fresh food. Full Feast recovery (30%
     max HP), plus each PC may equip one free Charm-slot item: a Lucky Fish
     Lure (+10 LCK, 1 battle only).

(3★) A bridge is guarded by a riddle-obsessed demon. [INT check] Any Mage or
     Elementalist PC auto-passes and the demon lets the party cross. On fail,
     combat begins — the demon occupies Column 1 alone and uses Guard stance,
     immune to Bleed and Poison.

(2★) A wandering merchant with a cart of oddities sells random Charm and Ring
     slot items at discount. One item in stock is always void-cursed (applies
     Burn value 3 per battle) but gives the highest stat bonus. [LCK check]
     A Rogue PC can identify the cursed item before purchasing.

(3★) An abandoned mine is full of void crystals. Mining them (safe, no combat)
     yields 2x Magic Bloom equivalent in raw void material — usable as a
     Magic Bloom substitute in any recipe. However, all PCs take Burn (value 4)
     at the start of the next combat from lingering void exposure.

(4★) The party is entered into a colosseum fight against captured demons.
     Three rounds, each against one demon type (standard, elite, champion).
     The colosseum crowd cheers for the party — a Bard PC passively generates
     a Valor Anthem (+25% ATK) effect for free in round 1 from the energy.

(2★) A library contains forbidden tomes on void magic. [INT check] A Mage or
     Elementalist who spends a full rest here gains a permanent +5 INT bonus.
     On fail (or any other class trying), the PC is Vulnerable at the start of
     the next battle from arcane overload.

(3★) An escaped experimental subject — clearly one of Zorath's creations —
     begs for mercy. Sparing it yields a free Phoenix Feather. Attacking it
     triggers combat: the creature has no aggro weight and will not attack
     back, but it has very high HP and must be defeated to continue.

(1★) A small chapel offers healing. A Cleric or High Priest PC who prays here
     has their Resurrection or Miracle talent CD reset to 0 before the next
     battle. Other PCs receive a standard 20 HP flat heal.

(4★) A treacherous mountain pass is laced with demonic ambushes in two
     consecutive combat nodes. Visibility is poor — all party members' Accuracy
     is reduced by -20% for both battles. Archers and Rogues with DEX 55+
     are immune to this penalty.

(2★) A bard composes an epic song about the party's deeds, but the lyrics
     contain uncomfortable references to their darker past. A Bard of Chaos PC
     who hears it is briefly Staggered (turn 1 of next combat) from recognition.
     All other PCs receive +5 CHA for the next two battles from the flattery.

(3★) A shared dream sequence visits all PCs simultaneously during a campground
     rest. Show each PC a vision of their demonic past. All PCs start the next
     battle with +15% damage (rage) but also Vulnerable (+50% damage taken,
     turn 1 only) as the dream lingers.

(4★) A fight breaks out in a city square as civilians watch in horror. The
     party must defeat enemies without letting any civilian cell (a designated
     neutral grid cell) take AoE damage. Mage and Bard of Chaos AoE skills
     risk hitting civilian cells — use them carefully or rely on single-target
     classes. Success yields +20 gold and a reputation bonus.

(5★) The Alliance's greatest champion challenges the party to single combat
     against their strongest PC. This is a 1v1 on a 1x3 mini-grid. The
     champion mirrors the chosen PC's highest attribute and has 200% of that
     PC's max HP. Winning grants a Legendary weapon for that PC's class slot.

(3★) A corrupted holy artifact is discovered. It burns demonic flesh — which
     means it deals Burn (value 15) per turn to any PC who equips it. The
     damage cannot be reduced by DEF or Block. However, it also grants +30 FTH
     while held. A Cleric with Purify (3 uses in combat) can cleanse it into a
     normal, powerful holy relic.

(2★) A village elder tells old legends that directly contradict the official
     Alliance history. The party gains a lore clue. [CHA check] A Bard who
     successfully charms the elder into sharing more details unlocks an extra
     story node on the map, replacing one combat node.

(4★) A portal to a demonic realm opens accidentally during a campground rest.
     The party is pulled through and must fight in an inverted battlefield —
     enemy and ally columns are swapped. Warriors and Knights who land on Column
     3 (their restricted zone) are Staggered for turn 1. Surviving closes the
     portal and rewards 2x Ancient Bone.

(5★) The party must decide whether to destroy or use Zorath's soul-forging
     machine. Using it: every PC gains +2 permanent TP but also permanently
     gains a Burn DoT (value 8) that activates in each battle and cannot be
     cleansed. Destroying it: no TP gain, but a hidden chapter 4 lane is
     unlocked and all Fallen Stacks on all PCs are cleared.

(3★) A group of paladins joins the party temporarily for two combat nodes.
     Each paladin functions as a bonus Cleric NPC (FTH 35, Minor Heal each
     turn). However, they grow suspicious — if any PC uses a void-related
     item or Burn-applying skill, the paladins abandon the party at the start
     of the next node.

(5★) Final revelation scene where all truths collide before the ultimate choice.
     Every PC's Fallen Stacks are revealed to the Alliance simultaneously.
     PCs with 0 Fallen Stacks gain the "Redeemed" passive (+20% ATK and DEF
     for the final battle). PCs with 2+ Fallen Stacks begin the final battle
     already in Berserk state, targeting randomly — and the entire party must
     survive the chaos before they can face Valthorak.
