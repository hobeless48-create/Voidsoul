1. GAMEPLAY FLOW & GAME LOOP
================================================================================
The game plays in distinct phases centered around party building, exploration,
and tactical turn-based combat.

- Tavern Lobby / Dashboard: The main hub where the player configures their character,
  loads/creates profiles, selects guild classes, manages talent points (TP), and 
  customizes their starting party (up to 4 heroes).
- Map Node Selection: The party moves lane by lane through 4 chapters: Chapter 1 Forest (8 lanes), Chapter 2 Dungeon Catacombs (12 lanes), Chapter 3 Volcanic Peak (10 lanes), and Chapter 4 Void Catacombs (10 lanes). In each lane, players choose between combat, elite combat, narrative story events (choices testing stats), campgrounds, or merchants.
- Battle Scene: Turn-based tactical combat on a 3x3 grid (for allies) facing a 3x3 grid of enemies.
- Camping / Feast: Rest phase after successful battles to heal the party (restores 30% of max HP), craft gear, buy/sell items, or deposit gold into the shared stash. The main menu widget is expanded by 20% to prevent scroll overflow. Recipes in the blacksmith forge are hidden until at least 1 unit of any ingredient in the recipe is gathered in the inventory.
- Victory / Defeat: Defeating the final boss of Chapter 4 completes the campaign.
  If all characters drop to 0 HP, defeat is encountered. Fallen characters gain 
  "Fallen Stacks" (up to 3 max; 4th stack results in permanent death).

2. SYSTEM STATS & FORMULAS
================================================================================

---------------------------
A. CORE ATTRIBUTES
---------------------------
- STR (Strength): Boosts physical damage, critical damage, and block chance.
- DEX (Dexterity): Boosts ranged scaling, accuracy, initiative, dodge, and crit chance.
- CON (Constitution): Main health attribute. Boosts defense, block chance, and block reduction.
- INT (Intelligence): Boosts magical spell damage and mana shield capacities.
- FTH (Faith): Boosts holy damage and healing multipliers.
- CHA (Charisma): Boosts support scaling, song effectiveness, and buff durations.
- LCK (Luck): Increases critical strike chance, dodge chance, and status effect triggers.

---------------------------
B. CLASS DAMAGE & HEALING SCALING FORMULAS
---------------------------
Active skill output uses a balanced flat + scale formula based on the character's base class and stats, then scales by the skill's damage/healing multiplier:
- Warrior:  Base = (12 + STR * 1.2 + CON * 0.6) * damageMultiplier
- Archer:   Base = (10 + DEX * 1.2 + STR * 0.5) * damageMultiplier
- Mage:     Base = (10 + INT * 1.5) * damageMultiplier
- Cleric (Damage): Base = (10 + FTH * 1.4) * damageMultiplier
- Rogue:    Base = (10 + DEX * 1.1 + LCK * 0.8) * damageMultiplier
- Bard (Damage): Base = (10 + CHA * 1.0 + LCK * 0.5) * damageMultiplier
- Cleric (Healing):
  * Single Ally: Healing = (10 + FTH * 1.0) * healingMultiplier
  * All Allies: Healing = (8 + FTH * 0.8) * healingMultiplier
- Cleric (Shield): Shelter Shield = 10 + FTH * 1.0
- Bard (Regen): Soothing Lute Regen = 5 + CHA * 0.4 per turn
- High Priest (Regen): Aura of Hope Regen = 10 + FTH * 0.8 per turn

AOE and Column penalties are applied to base damage values (90% for ALL_ENEMIES, 80% for COLUMN).

---------------------------
C. DERIVED STATS FORMULAS
---------------------------
At any point, character stats are calculated as:
- Max HP:          80 + (CON * 12) + ((Level - 1) * 15) + [Flat HP Bonuses]
- Crit Chance:     Min(80%, 5% + (DEX * 0.4) + (LCK * 0.5) + [Flat Crit Bonuses])
- Crit Damage:     150% + (STR * 0.8) + (DEX * 0.6) + (LCK * 1.2) + [Flat Crit Dmg Bonuses]
- Dodge Chance:    Min(75%, 3% + (DEX * 0.5) + (LCK * 0.3) + [Flat Dodge Bonuses])
- Block Chance:    Min(75%, 4% + (STR * 0.5) + (CON * 0.4))
- Block Reduction: Min(85%, 15% + (CON * 0.8))
- Min Initiative:  2 + Level + Math.floor(DEX / 3)
- Max Initiative:  10 + Level + Math.floor(DEX / 2)
- Accuracy:        95% + (DEX * 1) + (LCK * 0.5) + [Flat Accuracy Bonuses]
- Total Defense:   [Base Class Def] + (CON * 1) + [Flat Def Bonuses]

Base Defense by Class:
- Warrior / Inquisitor: 12
- Knight: 20
- Berserker / Sharpshooter / Duelist: 8
- Cleric / Bolt Slinger / Bard of Chaos / Assassin: 6
- Mage: 3
- Elementalist: 5
- Summoner / Rogue / Bard: 4
- High Priest / Bard of Valor: 10

================================================================================
3. COMBAT SYSTEMS
================================================================================

---------------------------
A. INITATIVE & TURN ORDER
---------------------------
At the start of battle, every participant rolls an Initiative value:
- Players roll: Random between Min Initiative and Max Initiative. Support classes 
  (Cleric, High Priest, Inquisitor, Bard, Bard of Valor, Bard of Chaos) receive +3.
- Enemies roll: Random between 0 and 10.
Participants are placed in descending order of rolled initiative.

---------------------------
B. GRID POSITIONING
---------------------------
- The battlefield features a 3x3 player grid (columns X, rows Y) and a 3x3 enemy grid.
- Column X: 1 = Frontline, 2 = Midline, 3 = Farthest Back.
- Row Y: 1 = Top, 2 = Center, 3 = Bottom.
- CONSTRAINT: Warrior classes (Warrior, Knight, Berserker) CANNOT stand on Column 3 
  due to melee attack range limits.

---------------------------
C. AGGRO SHARE / ENEMY TARGETING
---------------------------
If not taunted, enemies target player characters using weighted aggro probabilities:
- Base Aggro Weight: Warrior/Knight/Berserker = 40, Cleric/Priest/Inquisitor/Bard = 25, others = 20.
- Position Modifier: Characters standing on the frontline (Column 1) have their aggro 
  multiplied by 1.2.

---------------------------
D. DEFENSE, BLOCK, AND SHIELD MECHANICS
---------------------------
When a unit takes damage:
1. Target Def decreases the raw incoming damage flatly (Damage = Damage - Def, minimum 1).
2. If Block rolls successfully (using Block Chance), damage is reduced by Block Reduction.
3. If the target has a Shield buff, the shield absorbs incoming damage first.
4. Active "Guard" stance cuts incoming damage in half, and doubles Block Chance.
5. Fallen Stack penalty: Each Fallen Stack reduces damage dealt by 25% and reduces 
   healing/shielding received by 25%.

---------------------------
E. STATUS EFFECTS
---------------------------
- Burn: Deals damage equal to effect value at the start of the unit's turn.
- Poison: Deals damage equal to effect value at the start of the unit's turn.
- Bleed: Deals damage equal to effect value at the start of the unit's turn.
- Regen: Restores health equal to effect value.
- Taunt: Forces target to attack the taunter.
- Shield: Absorbs a flat amount of incoming damage.
- Stagger / Stun: Skips the unit's turn.
- Vulnerable: Target takes +50% damage.
- Crippled: Target deals -25% damage.

5. TALENT TREES SYSTEM
================================================================================
Players earn permanent Talent Points (TP) by leveling up (starting with 3 TP at Level 1, gaining +1 TP per level up). Each base class features a visual coordinate tree grid containing 10 customizable nodes.

- Point Pools: Talent Points (TP) and unlocked talent nodes are tracked separately for each class archetype. Swapping your active class in the lobby immediately swaps your talent configuration and unspent TP pool. Refunding talents correctly restores the spent points back into that class's TP pool.
- Node 1 (Iron Skin / Eagle Eye / Spark / Minor Heal / Nimble / Ballad): Free/Default (0 TP).
- Dynamic Node Costs: The purchase cost of any talent node scales dynamically:
  `Cost = Base Cost + number of already unlocked non-free nodes in that class`
  Base Cost is 0 TP for Node 1, 2 TP for Node 10 (Ultimate), and 1 TP for all other nodes. Therefore, each subsequent unlock in a class increases the TP cost of remaining nodes in that class by +1.
- Unlocking rule: A node can be purchased if you have sufficient TP and at least one parent node is already unlocked.
- All talent nodes are passives that grant flat stats, attribute bonuses, or amplify active skills and combat performance.

* Warrior Tree:
  - Iron Skin (war_t1_iron_skin): Unlocked by default (0 TP). Permanent +15 DEF.
  - Shield Strike Mastery (war_t2_power_strike): Shield Strike deals +20% damage and has +10% Critical Strike chance. Gains +5 STR.

7. NARRATIVE EVENTS & MULTIPLAYER VOTING
================================================================================
The campaign includes 100 narrative event cards. When a party lands on an 
"event" node, a story template is selected randomly.

- Stats Check: Many choices feature attribute-based checks (e.g. STR, DEX, INT, FTH, LCK, CHA). 
  The system queries the highest attribute value in the party to resolve the outcome.
- Rewards/Penalties: Choices yield gold, materials, experience points, status effects,
  permanent talent points, or trigger battles.
- Multiplayer Voting: In P2P multiplayer mode, every player in the room votes on their
  preferred choice. When at least 50% of the room has voted, a 5-second countdown triggers. 
  The choice with the highest tally is automatically resolved for the whole room.

9. RACE SYSTEM
================================================================================
The Race System permits players to select a starting Race for their primary character and companions during lobby customization. Each race offers unique tactical traits, passive team synergies, or significant trade-offs:

1. Human
   - Advantage: +1 Stat Point to use when leveling up (+4 Stat Points total per level, instead of 3).
   - Disadvantage: None.
2. Elf
   - Advantage: Crit Chance & Crit Damage +20% flat. 50% chance to double material drops from combat.
   - Disadvantage: Takes x2 damage from Poison status effect.
3. Dwarves
   - Advantage: +10% health (final calculation multiplier). Every 10 levels, crafted items gain +10% stats (integer floored).
   - Disadvantage: None.
4. Dragonborn
   - Advantage: +10% Strength. Defense increased by +20% or +8 flat (whichever is higher).
   - Disadvantage: Takes x2 damage from Bleed status effect.
5. Goblin
   - Advantage: +25% Gold gains (party benefit, does not stack). +1 Initiative roll.
   - Disadvantage: None.
6. Vampire
   - Advantage: Lifesteal: heals 20% of damage dealt from all attacks and counters.
   - Disadvantage: -20% final stats reduction (applied to core attributes).
7. Succubus
   - Advantage: +25% Charisma. Reduces narrative event stat check thresholds by 20% flat (party benefit, does not stack).
   - Disadvantage: -20% Max HP. Takes x2 damage from Burn status effect.
8. Dryad
   - Advantage: +20% outgoing healing. Team gains +10% EXP gained (party benefit, does not stack).
   - Disadvantage: Takes x2 damage from Burn status effect.
